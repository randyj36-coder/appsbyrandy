Orchid Guardian Acknowledgements

Orchid Guardian is built for Apple platforms and uses Apple-provided frameworks and services to support orchid records, local guidance, private iCloud sync, weather context, widgets, Apple Watch support, camera and photo features, light checks, notifications, and exports.

Apple Weather and WeatherKit

Weather information shown in Orchid Guardian is provided through Apple WeatherKit and Apple Weather services. Weather screens in the app include Apple Weather attribution and a link to Apple's weather data legal attribution page.

Apple Weather legal attribution: weatherkit.apple.com/legal-attribution.html.

Weather, temperature, humidity, and location-based orchid care notes are informational. They may not reflect the exact conditions around every plant, pot, window, room, greenhouse, or growing shelf.

Apple Services and Frameworks Used

App Interface

SwiftUI, UIKit, Foundation, Combine, Observation, CoreGraphics, and Charts are used to build the app interface, settings, dashboards, care cards, charts, and state updates.

Storage and Sync

SwiftData, CloudKit, and iCloud private database services are used for local app data and optional sync across the user's Apple devices.

Weather and Location

WeatherKit and CoreLocation are used to request local weather for the user's saved growing place or current location when the user chooses that option.

Photos and Camera

AVFoundation, PhotosUI, ImageIO, ARKit, SceneKit, and related Apple device features are used for camera, photo, visual inspection, light check, and image handling workflows.

Reminders and Background Work

UserNotifications and BackgroundTasks are used to support care reminders, daily care summaries, and background app maintenance where the device allows it.

Widgets and Apple Watch

WidgetKit and WatchConnectivity are used to support Orchid Guardian widgets and Apple Watch companion features.

Device Sensors

CoreMotion and other Apple device APIs may be used for device-aware checks and sensor-supported guidance where supported by the user's device.

Permissions and Availability

Camera, photo library, location, notifications, iCloud, widgets, Apple Watch, WeatherKit, and background features are controlled by Apple device settings and Apple service availability. Some features may be unavailable if permissions are denied, the device does not support the feature, iCloud is disabled, the Apple Watch companion app is not installed, or Apple services are unavailable.
